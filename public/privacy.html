Privacy

The keyword cannibalization checker finds pages on your website that compete for the same searches. This page explains what it reads, where that data goes and what it keeps.

Your API keys

Your Moz API token and Claude API key are stored in your own browser (session storage, or local storage if you tick "remember"). They are sent to this app's server only with your own requests, which forwards them to Moz or Anthropic and does not store or log them.

Google Search Console and Google Analytics

If you choose to connect Google, the app asks for read-only access to Search Console (webmasters.readonly) and Google Analytics (analytics.readonly), plus your email address so it can show which account is connected.

  • Your Google sign-in (a refresh token) is kept in an encrypted, HTTP-only cookie in your browser. The server does not store it anywhere else.
  • Search Console data (queries, pages, clicks, impressions, positions for the last 3 months) and GA4 data (sessions per page for the last 12 months) are read only while your library is being built. They are processed in memory, returned to your browser and never saved on the server.
  • Your library is saved in your browser only (IndexedDB). You can delete it any time in Settings, or download it as an Excel file.
  • Google data is used only to show you your own cannibalization report. It is not shared, sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect in Settings at any time, which revokes the app's access. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Claude

When you press "Ask Claude", the keywords, URLs, page titles and the numbers shown for them are sent to Anthropic's API with your own key, to write the recommendation. Anthropic's commercial terms apply to that request.

Site index

To know each page's title and publish date, the app crawls your public website (sitemap first, respecting robots.txt) and stores that public page list on the server so it can be reused for 30 days. It contains only what anyone can see on your public pages.

Cookies

The only cookies are the Google sign-in cookie described above and a short-lived one that protects the sign-in step. No analytics or advertising cookies are used.
